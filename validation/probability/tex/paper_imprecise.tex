% Imprecise (interval) reliability — the contribution. Data: validation/interval_sweep.csv (112 graphs,
% input interval half-width 0.3 per component). Reliability is monotone in every probability, so the
% EXACT range of each belief over the interval box is [belief(all-lower), belief(all-upper)], evaluated
% by CUDD at the two corners. "over-width" = how far a method's interval exceeds that exact range.
% IPA (with diamond conditioning) is EXACT; NAIVE interval propagation (no conditioning) over-widens
% because shared variables recur (the interval-arithmetic dependency problem). ROBDDs cannot propagate
% intervals natively at all.
% ============================================================================================

\begin{table}[t]
\centering
\caption{Exact interval reliability. Across 112 corpus graphs (per-component interval half-width $0.3$),
IPA's interval output equals the exact range $[\text{belief}(\text{lower}),\text{belief}(\text{upper})]$
to machine precision, whereas naive interval propagation (parents combined without diamond conditioning)
over-widens substantially. \emph{over-width} is the maximum per-node excess beyond the exact range;
\emph{unsound} is any failure to contain the exact range (0 = always sound).}
\label{tab:imprecise}
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}
\hline
Method & exact (\#/112) & over-width (min/median/max) & unsound \\
\hline
IPA (diamond conditioning) & \textbf{112/112} & $0$ / $0$ / $2.2\times10^{-16}$ & $0$ \\
Naive interval propagation & 0/112 & $0.062$ / $0.275$ / $0.452$ & $0$ \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\par\smallskip
\footnotesize Median exact belief range across the corpus is $0.44$; naive over-widening (median $0.275$)
is thus $\sim 60\%$ of the true range, and $111/112$ graphs are loose by $>0.1$. ROBDDs are omitted: they
require discretising each interval into many point evaluations rather than a single exact propagation.
\end{table}

(random $n{=}12$ DAG). The IPA p-box band (blue) tightly brackets the true CDF (black, Monte Carlo); the

reliability has none (e.g. at $x{=}0.24$ the true CDF is $0.71$ but the naive band is $[0,0.015]$),
